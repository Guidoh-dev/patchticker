## What changed

- We upgraded our Desktop client to using Electron 42, which aside from general maintenance best practices on our end, yielded some incremental improvements to CPU usage of our app.
- We've made language and style updates across several settings pages, including Data & Privacy and Activity Privacy.
- Activity is now Games & Apps
- Content & Social is now Messaging Permissions
- Related settings, such as Authorized Apps/Connections and OS-specific pages/keybinds, have been consolidated for clarity.
- General: Resolved an issue on Desktop where changing your email could fail after completing a CAPTCHA, leaving you unable to update your email address.
- General: Fixed a bug on Desktop where having the overlay enabled could turn your entire client for certain games black when tabbed out.
- General: Fixed a bug on Desktop where the overlay's chat widget couldn't be dragged to reposition it when a server was selected.
- General: Fixed an issue on Desktop where dragging a server icon in the overlay could accidentally grab the image and leave the chat widget stuck to your cursor.
- General: Fixed an issue on Desktop where creating a group DM from the game overlay would open the modal in the main Discord window instead of the overlay.
- General: Resolved a bug on Desktop where long server names were cut off in the profile Activity card when you joined a voice channel.
- Chat: Fixed an issue on Desktop where pasting a large number of emojis at once could cause the app to freeze or crash.
