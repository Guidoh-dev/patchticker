## What changed

- In early June, we shipped the iOS migration to React Native's New Architecture, Fabric. We're measuring almost 20% wins in typical app initialization times from this effort. This also should unlock some larger scale projects to help us improve reliability and performance across both Android and iOS in the future.
- Looping GIFs and videos should now properly loop on Linux. We don't want to write an entire engineering blog about this, but the fix for this issue was pretty deep in the stack. I promise it wasn't something stupid like "oh, we didn't set `loop` to 1 on Linux.”
- Have you ever wanted to forward a message to someone’s DMs at 3am and had to deliberate on whether or not to ping them while they’re asleep or Copy/Paste the message itself so you could send it with @silent-ly? Worry no more: message forwarding now supports @silent.
- Years ago, we introduced a feature specifically for Community-enabled servers called Channel Pinning, which allowed you to move your favorite channels to the top of the channel list, just for yourself. It's now available on all servers, not just Community-enabled ones. Enjoy!
- We made a small improvement to our start session logic that reduced p95/p99 session connection times by about 100ms. We literally just flipped a boolean in a config to yield this, and didn't lose anything meaningful from the swap. (I’m always saying “I flip booleans in configs to yield stuff.”)
- Chat: Fixed an issue on Desktop where opening a channel where the most recent messages were all from blocked users could cause a JS crash.
- Chat: Fixed a bug on Desktop where applying a spelling suggestion in Text in Voice chat didn't work while in fullscreen call view.
- Audio/Video: Fixed a bug on Desktop where clicking a game's title in a voice popout with rich presence wouldn't open the game details after the first try.
- Audio/Video: Resolved a bug on Desktop where the voice user popover showed an "Ask to Join" button for an embedded activity that was actually open to everyone. It now correctly shows "Join.”
- Audio/Video: Fixed a bug on Desktop where the avatars in the voice channel facepile were misaligned, sitting at the bottom of the pill instead of centered.
- Audio/Video: Resolved an issue on Desktop where clicking a user's icon in a collapsed voice channel didn't open their profile popout.
- Audio/Video: Resolved a bug on Desktop where several guild sidebar popovers in the voice experience displayed with incorrect padding, spacing, and text alignment.
